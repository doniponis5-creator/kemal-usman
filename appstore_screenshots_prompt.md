## Design Notes
- All text in **Russian**
- Currency: **сом** (Kyrgyz som)
- The app is a perfume e-commerce store based in Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan
- Target audience: young women and men 18-35 who love luxury fragrances
- The overall feel should be: Apple-level polish, luxury fashion brand, premium but accessible
- No gradients on UI elements inside the phone — keep the app UI clean and flat
- Phone bezels should be thin, modern iPhone 15 Pro style
- Background behind phone: can use subtle bokeh, gold dust particles, or smooth dark gradients
- Make sure headline text is large enough to read at App Store thumbnail size
